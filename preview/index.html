Preview

Guide › Preview

Arranging widgets is easier when they show real numbers. Preview runs a pretend ride on every overlay, so you can see what each one looks like mid-workout (countdowns, splits with stars, a cadence cue, scorecards) and place and size them as usual.

Nothing is recorded, no ride is saved, and the trainer isn’t touched: your devices stay connected and idle.

Start it

Section titled “Start it”

Control window → Overlay → Preview:

  1. Pick the Ride:
    • Intervals demo (16 minutes): a warm-up, four work blocks with recoveries, a cadence dip (“Spin up now”) and a fading effort, so every overlay has something to show;
    • Steady Z2 (sample ride): a 52-minute steady endurance ride, replayed;
    • Selected workout: the workout selected in the Library, with a pretend rider following its targets.
  2. Pick the Speed: 1× (real time) or 4×.
  3. Press Preview overlay.

Also from the menu-bar icon (Preview overlay) and the phone’s Overlay tab (there the third choice is Last workout, the one you rode last).

While it runs, the main card shows a PREVIEW tag and the Control window’s header says “Preview running · End preview”. It loops until you end it.

Arrange while it runs

Section titled “Arrange while it runs”

Everything works as usual: switch presets, turn widgets on and off, change sizes, press Ctrl+Shift+H and drag things around. Your changes are saved like any other layout change.

End it

Section titled “End it”

End preview (Overlay tab, the header, the menu-bar icon, the phone), or hold the card’s End button. Starting a real workout ends the preview first; during a real ride, or while mirroring, Preview isn’t available.

A computer mirroring this one never sees the preview.
