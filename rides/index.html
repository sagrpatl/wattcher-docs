Rides and .FIT files

Guide › Rides and .FIT files

Every ride is saved as a .fit file, the standard activity format that Strava, intervals.icu, Garmin Connect, TrainingPeaks and others import. It has second-by-second power, cadence, heart rate and an estimated speed and distance, one lap per interval, and your FTP.

The Rides tab

Section titled “The Rides tab”

Control window → Rides lists your rides, newest first. Click one for its summary:

  • Time (moving time; pauses are listed separately), Avg power, NP, IF, TSS, Work (kJ), Cadence, Heart rate, Distance (estimated from your power and weight);
  • a chart of your power over the workout’s profile;
  • one row per interval: time, target, your average (green on target, blue under, red over), max power, cadence and heart rate. Skipped or restarted intervals are tagged.

Show in Finder shows the .fit file; Delete removes the ride and its file (it asks first).

Where the files are

Section titled “Where the files are”

~/Documents/TrainerOverlay/Rides, named like 2026-10-18_1603_tempo-openers.fit. Settings → Recording → Open rides folder (or the Rides tab’s button) opens it.

Rides shorter than a minute are thrown away, unless Settings → Recording → Keep rides shorter than 1 minute is on.

Upload to Strava (or elsewhere)

Section titled “Upload to Strava (or elsewhere)”

Wattcher doesn’t upload by itself. To add a ride to Strava:

  1. On strava.com, click + (top right) → Upload activity → File.
  2. Choose the ride’s .fit file from ~/Documents/TrainerOverlay/Rides (drag it from Finder after Show in Finder).
  3. Strava reads it as an indoor ride with your power, heart rate and laps. Give it a title and Save & View.

intervals.icu (Activities → Upload), Garmin Connect (Import Data) and TrainingPeaks (Upload) work the same way.

If Wattcher stops mid-ride

Section titled “If Wattcher stops mid-ride”

Rides are written to a journal as you ride. If Wattcher (or the computer) stops before the ride ends, the next launch shows a banner about the unfinished ride: Save ride turns it into a .fit file, Discard throws it away.
