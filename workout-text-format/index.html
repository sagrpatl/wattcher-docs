Workout text format (intervals.icu syntax)

Guide › Building workouts › Text format reference

The Builder tab and .txt files in the Workouts folder use the intervals.icu workout builder text syntax, so you can paste workouts between intervals.icu and Wattcher. This page is the full reference; Building workouts is the short version.

Sources for the syntax: David Tinker’s Workout builder post, the Workout Builder Syntax Quick Guide, the text events announcement, and forum replies on ranges vs. ramps. intervals.icu has no formal grammar; where it is unclear, the choice made here is noted.

Steps

Section titled “Steps”

A step is a line starting with -:

Durations

Section titled “Durations”
Form Meaning
1h, 10m, 30s, 90s hours / minutes / seconds
1h2m30s, 5m30s, 1h5s combined (largest unit first)
1m30 1 min 30 s (a trailing bare number is the next smaller unit)
1h30 1 h 30 min (same rule; intervals.icu may not accept it)
5', 30", 1'30", 1'30 short forms
10min, 2mins, 45sec spelled-out units
1.5m, 37.5s decimals

m always means minutes. Distances (2km, 500mtr, 1mi, 400y) are errors: a trainer workout needs time-based steps.

Power targets (one per step)

Section titled “Power targets (one per step)”
Form Meaning
75%, 75% FTP % of FTP
200W, 200w, 200 W absolute watts, converted to % of your FTP when the file is loaded
Z2, z2 power zone (see table below), steady at the zone midpoint
88-92%, 88%-92%, 95–105% range: steady at the midpoint (90 %)
200-240W, Z3-Z4 ranges in watts / zones: also steady at the midpoint
ramp 50-75%, Ramp 75%-50% ramp: target moves linearly from the first to the second value
ramp 100-200W, ramp Z1-Z3 ramps in watts, or between zone midpoints
ramp Z2 ramp across one zone, low to high bound (56 → 75 %)
freeride, free ride no target: ERG off, the trainer runs in resistance mode
maxeffort, max effort all-out effort, no target. Extension: not intervals.icu syntax (from .zwo MaxEffort)

Gears and resistance. Without a target you shift 24 virtual gears (− / +; how they feel: calibrated gears). A freeride or maxeffort step can start in a gear (- 15s maxeffort gear 18) or at an exact trainer resistance level (- 3m freeride resistance 40%, 0–100 %). Either carries on into later free steps until you shift (from an exact level, − / + go to the nearest gear below / above it) or a step names another. Extension: not intervals.icu syntax; gear / resistance on a step with a power target, or both on one step, is an error.

Sprint steps. - 10s sprint is an all-out step (like maxeffort) whose resistance is set from your own sprints. Every rider’s power peaks at a different cadence and fades at a different rate, so Wattcher learns both from your sprints and max efforts (Settings → Advanced → Your sprint). The first sprint starts light; within two or three it starts where you settle near your best cadence. In the step it holds the level for 2 s while you wind up, eases off as you tire, and never adds resistance in the last 3 s. - 10s sprint 120rpm keeps you from dropping below 120 rpm. A step’s resistance 40% is where it starts. Shifting during the step takes over from it. sprint is cue text before the duration or on a step with a power target (- 30s Sprint 150%). Extension: not intervals.icu syntax.

Ranges vs. ramps. In intervals.icu a range without ramp is a target range, and “ERG mode and some devices use the midpoint of the range” (forum, Gerald). ERG can only hold one number, so we ride the midpoint. Only ramp makes a slope.

Zones. Default intervals.icu / Coggan power zones (% FTP):

Zone Z1 Z2 Z3 Z4 Z5 Z6 Z7
Range 45–55* 56–75 76–90 91–105 106–120 121–150 151–200*
Target 50 65.5 83 98 113 135.5 175.5

* Z1 and Z7 are open-ended in the zone model. A bounded range gives a usable ERG target. Custom zones (CZ1) are errors.

Cadence

Section titled “Cadence”

90rpm, 90 rpm, 85-95rpm (the midpoint, 90, is shown as the cadence target). Values must be between 1 and 250.

Cue text

Section titled “Cue text”
  • Text before the duration is the step’s cue (- Recovery 30s 50% shows “Recovery”).
  • Other words that aren’t syntax, such as - 5m recovery at 40% or - 4m 100% 40-50 rpm, power is less important, are appended to that cue.
  • Timed cues (intervals.icu text events): N^D text shows text N seconds into the step for D seconds. N^ text omits the duration.
    <!> ends the cue text explicitly. Everything before it is cue text, even 5m or 90%. Use it when the cue mentions durations or targets. A cue later than the end of its step gives a warning.

Intensity

Section titled “Intensity”

intensity=warmup|cooldown|active|recovery (also work, interval, rest) sets the segment’s role. The role drives HUD labels and “intensity applies to work only”. Without it: steps in a section whose name contains “warm” / “cool” are warm-up / cool-down. Free ride counts as recovery and max effort as work. Any other step counts as work above 75 % FTP, otherwise recovery. An unknown value gives a warning and is ignored.

Sections and repeats

Section titled “Sections and repeats”
  • Any other non-blank line is a section header (Warmup, Main Set, Cooldown:). Its name labels the steps below it.
  • Nx anywhere in a header repeats the steps below it: Main Set 3x, 3x, 2x Over-unders.
  • A section ends at the next blank line or the next header. As in intervals.icu, leave a blank line before and after each repeat block. Steps after a blank line without a new header belong to no section.
  • Nested repeats are not supported (same as intervals.icu). A second Nx in a header is an error. 0x is an error. A repeat with no steps below it gives a warning.

Comments and metadata

Section titled “Comments and metadata”
  • Lines starting with #, //, |, <, ---, ***, ___ or === are ignored. This covers Markdown headings, tables, rules and HTML, which intervals.icu also ignores.
  • # name: …, # author: … and # description: … set the workout’s metadata (repeat # description: for more lines). intervals.icu treats these lines as Markdown headings.

Not supported (errors)

Section titled “Not supported (errors)”
Syntax Why
2km, 500mtr, 1mi, 400y distance steps: a trainer workout needs durations
70% HR, 95% LTHR, Z2 HR, 140-150bpm heart-rate targets: ERG needs power
60% Pace, Z2 Pace, 5:00/km Pace pace targets (run / swim)
60% MMP 5m % of mean-max power
3 W/kg W/kg targets
CZ1 custom zones
a step without a target use freeride for “no ERG”
200W without an FTP watts need your FTP (Settings)

HR or pace only cause an error right after a target (60% HR). In other places they are ordinary cue text (- 10m 70% steady pace, keep HR low). intervals.icu-only features with no trainer meaning are ignored: “Press lap”, localised cues (en/… fr/…) and Markdown styling.

Errors and warnings

Section titled “Errors and warnings”

The parser reports every error with a line, column and message. For example, line 3:3: distance "5km" isn't supported on the trainer. A file with errors does not load. Warnings (an empty repeat, a cue past its step’s end, an unknown intensity) do not stop loading.

Editing .zwo, .erg and .mrc workouts

Section titled “Editing .zwo, .erg and .mrc workouts”

Edit on a Library workout opens it in the Builder as this text: identical runs of steps become Nx blocks, and warm-up / cool-down steps go under Warmup / Cooldown headers, so no intensity= keywords are needed. Saving keeps your original file and writes a new .txt workout. The optional .zwo export drops section names.

For developers

Section titled “For developers”

Parser and serializer: crates/core/src/workout/icu.rs (parse_icu, to_icu; parse_icu(to_icu(w)) == w holds for every fixture; with_section_labels moves warm-up / cool-down segments under their headers; to_zwo for the export). Example files: crates/core/tests/fixtures/workouts/icu_*.txt. See also the architecture notes.
