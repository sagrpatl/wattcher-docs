Pair your trainer and sensors

Guide › Pair your trainer and sensors

Wattcher talks Bluetooth to:

  • smart trainers that speak the standard FTMS protocol (Wahoo KICKR and most current trainers): power, cadence, speed, and ERG control;
  • power meters (pedals, cranks) and cadence sensors;
  • heart-rate sensors: chest straps and watches that broadcast heart rate;
  • Zwift Click shifters: the two paddles shift gears.

Only the trainer is needed. Everything is remembered: next time your devices reconnect by themselves as soon as they’re on and nearby.

Add your trainer

Section titled “Add your trainer”
  1. Wake the trainer: pedal a few strokes (trainers fall asleep after a few minutes).
  2. Close other apps that may be connected to it: Zwift, SYSTM, the Wahoo app. Some trainers accept only one app at a time.
  3. Control window → Devices → Search for devices.
  4. Your trainer appears under Nearby, grouped by kind. Press Use as Trainer.

The header of the Control window then shows its name with a green light, and the numbers at the top of the Devices tab come alive.

(This screenshot shows the simulated trainer used to make the guide. With real devices, each one is listed with its status, and the ones nearby with Use as … buttons.)

Power meters paired to the trainer. If your pedals are already paired to the trainer (the KICKR can use them to run ERG), you don’t need to add them in Wattcher: the trainer reports that power. Add the pedals separately only if you want Wattcher to show and record their power instead; Wattcher prefers a separate power meter over the trainer’s power, and crank cadence over the trainer’s.

Changing a device. Use as … on another device asks whether to replace the current one. Forget stops using a device (you can add it again any time).

No trainer yet? Settings → Advanced → Trainer connection → Simulated trainer makes up numbers, so you can try everything without hardware.

Add a heart-rate sensor (optional)

Section titled “Add a heart-rate sensor (optional)”

Heart rate is shown on the overlay and the phone, saved with every ride, and used by the heart-rate cap (see Off-target cues).

  1. Chest strap (Polar H10, Wahoo TICKR, Garmin HRM-Dual / HRM-Pro): put it on with damp electrodes and tighten it. Straps only broadcast while worn. Watch: turn on heart-rate broadcast (below).
  2. Devices → Search for devices (or Search on the “Add a heart-rate sensor” card).
  3. Press Use as Heart rate.

The Devices tab shows “Skin contact ✓” when a strap reports good contact, or “No skin contact · moisten the electrodes and tighten the strap”.

Watches

Section titled “Watches”
Watch How to broadcast
Suunto (Race S and others) Start an exercise with HR broadcast / “Share HR” turned on in the exercise options. It broadcasts only during that exercise. Verified with a Suunto Race S.
Garmin Broadcast Heart Rate (or “Broadcast HR during activity”)
Polar, Coros, Whoop The heart-rate broadcast setting in the watch or its app
Apple Watch Doesn’t broadcast heart rate to other devices: use a strap

ANT+-only straps (some older Garmin straps) don’t work: Wattcher uses Bluetooth only.

Tips

Section titled “Tips”
  • Polar H10 and most straps accept two Bluetooth connections at once (the H10: 2). If it’s already connected to a watch, a phone and another app, it stops showing up: disconnect it there, or turn that device’s Bluetooth off.
  • Keep the sensor within a few metres of the computer.
  • Still not listed? Turn on Show all Bluetooth devices under Nearby: some watches don’t say they’re a heart-rate sensor until they’re connected.
  • The Devices tab’s I don’t see my device has this list too.

Add Zwift Click shifters (optional)

Section titled “Add Zwift Click shifters (optional)”

The paddles do what the overlay’s − / + do: the left paddle shifts down a gear (easier), the right paddle shifts up (harder). In ERG they nudge the target by 1 %. The other buttons do nothing yet.

  1. Close Zwift if it’s connected to the Clicks.
  2. Press a button on each unit to wake it.
  3. Devices → Search for devices. They’re listed under Shifters, both as “Zwift Click”.
  4. Press Use as Shifter on each. Add both: the left unit passes the right unit’s presses on, but with both added either one keeps working if the other is asleep.

The Clicks sleep after a few minutes without a press; pressing a button wakes them and they reconnect by themselves.
