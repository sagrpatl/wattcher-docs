Phone remote

Guide › Phone remote

Ride far from the computer? Your phone or tablet becomes a remote with big buttons: see the ride, pause, skip, make it harder or easier, start workouts and arrange the overlay, without leaving the bike. It’s a web page served by Wattcher on your home network, so there’s nothing to install on the phone.

Connect the phone to the computer that talks to the trainer. To show the ride on a second computer as well (say, the TV), see Mirroring.

Pair your phone

Section titled “Pair your phone”
  1. Control window → Settings → Phone & mirroring → turn on Control the ride from a phone or tablet.
  2. Point the phone’s camera at the QR code and open the link (or Copy link and send it to yourself). The phone must be on the same Wi-Fi as the computer.
  3. The Control window’s header shows “1 phone connected”.

  • Keep the link to yourself: anyone on your network with it can control the ride, start workouts and change the overlay, FTP and sounds. New link… makes a new one and disconnects every phone (use it if you shared the link by mistake). The phone page can’t reach your devices, files or ride files, or quit the app.
  • Firewall: the first time, macOS may ask whether Wattcher may accept incoming network connections: choose Allow. On Linux with a firewall, open the port (see Troubleshooting).
  • Port: 8787 by default (the next free one if it’s taken); change it under Network port only if another app uses it.
  • Home screen: “Add to Home Screen” (Safari’s Share menu, Chrome’s menu) opens it full screen like an app.
  • Keep the phone awake: tap the “Tap to keep the screen awake” banner once per visit, or raise the phone’s auto-lock time.

The four tabs

Section titled “The four tabs”

Ride: target and 3-second power (coloured by the power badge), interval time left, cadence with the cadence lane, heart rate, interval average, the next interval, and the profile with your power so far. Big buttons: − / + (harder / easier, or shift gears with no ERG target; the buttons grow while you shift), +30 s, ERG, Back, Pause / Resume, Skip, and Hold to end workout (press and hold 1 s; a quick tap asks “End ride? Yes / No” instead). Next to it, a button with the overlay preset’s name switches to the next preset. Cues and toasts show here too (Skip → Undo).

When the ride is over, the Ride tab shows the summary: time, average power, NP, IF, TSS, work, cadence, heart rate, “Saved ✓” and each interval’s target against your average, with Ride again and Choose workout.

Workouts: your Library with mini profiles and a search box, and at the top your last ride and Ride again. Tap a workout for its profile, duration, intervals and estimated TSS / IF at your FTP, then Start. If a ride is already running it asks first (“End current ride and start this?”, hold 1 s).

Overlay: everything from the Control window’s Overlay tab, sized for a thumb: Preview, presets (Next preset, Save as preset…, Reset preset / Reset sizes, and your presets with rename / Update / delete / In cycle), each overlay on or off, and for the selected overlay its size, its options, a D-pad to nudge it (tap for one step, hold to keep moving; Fine 2 px / Normal 10 px / Coarse 50 px) and a 3×3 screen map to snap it to a corner, an edge or the center. Below: the overall size, show / hide, and on macOS “Stay on top of”. Changes show on the computer’s screen right away.

More: trainer status and phones connected, who controls the trainer, FTP (±1 / ±5 or type it; applies right away, also mid-ride), sounds on / off, volume and Test, cue options, mirroring requests to answer, first-steps help, and the app version.

Changes made on the phone show up in the Control window (and on other phones), and the other way round. The keyboard shortcuts and the overlay keep working as usual.

The phone opens on the tab you used last, or on Ride while a ride is running.
