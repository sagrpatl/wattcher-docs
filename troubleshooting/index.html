Troubleshooting

Guide › Troubleshooting

Bluetooth and devices

Section titled “Bluetooth and devices”

“Bluetooth isn’t available” / the trainer isn’t found (macOS). System Settings → Privacy & Security → Bluetooth → turn on Wattcher. Wattcher is signed on your own Mac only, so after an update macOS may ask again: allow it. (If you started the app from Terminal instead of /Applications, the permission belongs to Terminal.)

The trainer isn’t listed. Pedal a few strokes to wake it, keep it within a few metres, press Search for devices again. Make sure no other app is connected to it (next item).

Another app is controlling the trainer (Zwift, SYSTM, the Wahoo app). Only one app can run ERG at a time. Wattcher shows a banner: choose Just show my data (Wattcher shows your numbers and the targets, the other app sets the resistance) for this ride, or close the other app. If you always ride with the other app, pick Just show my data in Settings. On some trainers the other app must disconnect completely before Wattcher can connect.

ERG doesn’t hold the target (power follows your cadence). Check the card for NO ERG and the ERG button: Wattcher was set to Just show my data, so nothing sets the resistance. Press ERG, or set Settings → Riding back to Wattcher.

Heart-rate sensor not found. Straps broadcast only while worn with damp electrodes; watches need heart-rate broadcast on; most straps take two connections at most. See Pair your trainer and sensors. Numbers that a sensor isn’t sending show “—”.

A sensor drops out mid-ride. Wattcher reconnects by itself and shows a note on the overlay; the ride keeps going (the workout clock pauses if power stops, as in auto-pause).

The overlay

Section titled “The overlay”

The overlay is hidden behind a game. Settings → Overlay → Stay on top of → Fullscreen games, or Everything if it’s still hidden (needed for CrossOver / Wine games). Games in exclusive fullscreen can’t be drawn over at all: set the game to borderless or windowed fullscreen. See The overlay.

The overlay is gone. Ctrl+Shift+O shows it again (or the menu-bar icon → Show overlays). Off the screen after unplugging a display? Overlay tab → Place → a spot on the grid, or Reset preset.

I can’t click the overlay’s buttons. Hover the main card first: the control bar appears and only its buttons take the mouse. Widgets never take the mouse, except while arranging (Ctrl+Shift+H).

The overlay doesn’t react to the mouse / I can’t drag it. Press Ctrl+Shift+H to arrange; press it again to lock.

Sound

Section titled “Sound”

No sound. Settings → Sounds: Sound cues on, volume up, Test. Ctrl+Shift+M may have muted it. Sounds play through the computer’s current output device.

Phone or mirroring can’t connect

Section titled “Phone or mirroring can’t connect”
  • Phone and computer must be on the same network (not a guest Wi-Fi that isolates devices).
  • macOS: allow Wattcher in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network, and under Network → Firewall → Options (incoming connections).
  • Linux with firewalld: sudo firewall-cmd --add-port=8787/tcp for the phone, plus --add-service=mdns for mirroring (add --permanent to keep it).
  • The phone says “Link expired”: someone pressed New link…; scan the new QR code.
  • Using another port than 8787? Settings → Phone & mirroring → Network port shows the one in use.
  • Settings says “update Wattcher there to 1.2” next to a computer: that computer runs Wattcher 1.1, which can’t mirror or be mirrored by a newer one. Update it; the pairing keeps working with nothing to pair again.

Linux

Section titled “Linux”
  • Overlay missing or half drawn: on NVIDIA don’t set WEBKIT_DISABLE_DMABUF_RENDERER=1 (Wattcher already sets what it needs). On GNOME, or if the overlay doesn’t appear at all, Settings → Overlay → Overlay mode → XWayland and restart Wattcher.
  • Shortcuts do nothing: they need Wattcher’s launcher entry (use the Flatpak and start Wattcher from the app launcher; the AppImage alone has none) and the one-time Global Shortcuts Requested dialog (KDE: OK). Check or change them in System Settings → Keyboard → Shortcuts → Wattcher.
  • Bluetooth: make sure Bluetooth is on (bluetoothctl show); no permission is needed.

Settings, logs and quitting

Section titled “Settings, logs and quitting”

The bottom of Settings shows which Wattcher you run (here 1.0.0); the CHANGELOG says what changed since.

  • Settings and data: ~/Library/Application Support/dev.trainer-overlay.app (macOS) or ~/.local/share/dev.trainer-overlay.app (Linux): settings.json, devices.json, ride journals. Rides and workouts are in ~/Documents/TrainerOverlay. Reinstalling keeps all of it.
  • Logs: each ride writes a second-by-second log to the logs folder inside the settings folder (ride-YYYYmmdd-HHMMSS.log), handy when something felt wrong mid-ride. Everything else the app does (mode switches and where they came from, setting changes, connects and drops) goes to a daily app-YYYYmmdd.log there, kept for a week. Copy diagnostics at the bottom of Settings copies the version, the main settings (never links or passwords) and the recent log, ready to paste; Show logs opens the folder. Settings → Advanced → Show technical details adds device ids, signal strength and raw error messages to the app.
  • Quit: the Quit button, the menu-bar icon → Quit Wattcher, or Ctrl+Shift+Q (a ride in progress is ended and saved, the trainer released). Closing the Control window only hides it.
